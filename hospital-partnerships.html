For NICU teams and hospitals

Let's make support useful for families.

We welcome conversations with NICU social workers, family-support coordinators, volunteer services teams, and hospital community-benefit staff.

A collaborative approach

Hospital guidance shapes the work.

Every NICU has its own policies, routines, and family needs. Before preparing or delivering care packages, we want to understand what your team considers appropriate and helpful.

Early conversations with hospital teams are helping us shape the initiative around real needs and facility requirements.

We have made some great contacts and would love to expand our hospital outreach!

What we'd like to learn before donating to your hospital:

  • Which items are permitted for family care packages?
  • Are there food, fragrance, fabric, or packaging restrictions?
  • Which families or caregivers should receive packages?
  • What quantity and delivery schedule would be useful?
  • Who should coordinate delivery and distribution?
A simple process

Start with a conversation.

01 · Connect

Tell us about your unit

Share the best point of contact and the type of support your team is open to exploring.

02 · Plan

Agree on what works

Confirm package contents, quantities, packaging, delivery logistics, and any approval steps.

03 · Coordinate

Keep communication open

Review what was useful and adjust future deliveries based on feedback from the team.
