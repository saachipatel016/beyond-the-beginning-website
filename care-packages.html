Our first community initiative

A little care for the people caring so much.

Thoughtfully assembled care packages for parents and caregivers spending time in the NICU—created to offer practical comfort and a reminder that they are not alone.

The idea

Care for parents, too.

When a baby is in the NICU, parents may spend long hours at the hospital while navigating uncertainty and unfamiliar routines. A small package cannot change that experience, but it can offer a practical item, a moment of comfort, or a kind reminder that someone is thinking of them.

Designed with families in mind

Practical. Gentle. Thoughtful.

The goal is not to give more things. It is to offer the right things, with care.

What may be included

Useful comforts, selected carefully.

Final package contents will depend on the receiving hospital's approval, infection-control guidance, and the needs families identify.

♡

Everyday comfort

Hospital-approved personal comfort items such as cozy socks, a small notebook, or other practical essentials.

✎

Thoughtful encouragement

A handwritten note, a gentle reminder to care for themselves, or a simple way to record questions and thoughts.

☕

Practical support

Where permitted, individually packaged snacks or a modest food or beverage gift card suitable for the hospital setting.

Hospital guidance comes first. Items such as food, lotions, fabrics, fragrances, reusable drinkware, and products intended for babies may be restricted. We will not distribute items to a unit without confirming its requirements first.
How distribution works

Built around each hospital's process.

1

Listen

Connect with the NICU team to understand family needs, permitted items, and preferred package quantities.

2

Prepare

Assemble packages according to the hospital's guidance and agreed distribution process.

3

Deliver

Coordinate delivery with the designated hospital contact. We do not approach families or enter clinical areas without permission.
