Privacy

Respect for family privacy comes first.

This version of the website is designed as a static information site and does not include visitor accounts, AI chat, or on-site submission forms.

Information you share

This website does not intentionally collect personal information through forms. If you contact Beyond the Beginning using an email address or another external service, that service will process your information under its own privacy terms.

Website hosting

The website is hosted through Cloudflare Pages. The hosting provider may process technical information such as IP addresses and request logs as part of operating and securing the service. Refer to Cloudflare's privacy information for details.

Family and patient information

Please do not send patient names, dates of birth, medical records, images, or private clinical details. Do not share a family's story or photograph publicly without informed permission and any required authorization.

External links

Links to other organizations are provided for convenience. Those websites have their own privacy practices and content.

This is a general website notice, not legal advice. Review it against the site's actual hosting, communication, and operational practices before launch.
