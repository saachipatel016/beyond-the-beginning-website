Beyond the Beginning shares general educational information and links to outside resources. It does not provide medical care.

Not medical advice. This website cannot diagnose a baby, interpret symptoms or monitor readings, recommend treatment, or determine whether a baby is ready for discharge.

Talk with your baby's care team

For questions about an individual baby's condition, symptoms, treatment, feeding, equipment, or care plan, contact the baby's qualified healthcare team. For urgent concerns, follow the hospital's emergency instructions or contact local emergency services.

External information

Medical information can change, and outside resources may not apply to every family or situation. Unless a page specifically names a qualified reviewer and review date, do not assume the content has been clinically reviewed.

Hospital care packages

Items in care packages are subject to the receiving hospital's approval and policies. Nothing intended for a baby should be distributed or used without the hospital's explicit guidance.
