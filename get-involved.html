A few important boundaries

Support should always protect families.

  • Do not request, collect, or share patient names, medical records, photographs, or private clinical details.
  • Do not enter hospital units or approach families without the hospital's permission.
  • Do not give medical advice or present general information as individualized guidance.
  • Do not promise volunteer hours, tax deductions, or charitable status unless those details are formally confirmed.
